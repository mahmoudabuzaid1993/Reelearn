## Key Question 1: Company facts (founders, founding, HQ, entities, headcount, key people)

### Takeaway
Deepstash was founded in 2019 in Bucharest by Vladimir Oane (CEO), Dan Ciotu and Cristian Mezei (ex-uberVU team, sold to Hootsuite in 2014). It operates through a Romanian entity (Deepstash SRL) and a US entity (Brainstash, Inc; LinkedIn lists Newark, Delaware as primary office). In October 2026 the company rebranded its corporate identity to "Smartheads", a family of five apps (Deepstash, Paladin, Protocol, Dilemmo, Empirio), with ~59 staff on LinkedIn.

## Key Question 3: Business model and pricing (Pro tiers, free limits, trials, lifetime deals, B2B, ads)

### Takeaway
Deepstash is a freemium consumer subscription app ("Deepstash Pro") monetized mainly via in-app subscriptions (US list: $12.99/month, $29.99/3 months, and yearly SKUs from $49.99 to $119.99 with a 7-day trial on yearly), supplemented by in-feed ads in the free tier. Lifetime plans have only appeared as limited-time StackSocial-style promotions ($119.97–$149.99 in 2023). No team/enterprise/API product exists; a cross-app "Smartheads" bundle is announced as "coming soon".
