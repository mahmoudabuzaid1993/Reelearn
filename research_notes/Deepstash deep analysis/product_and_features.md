# Deepstash: Product and Features Profile (observed 2026-10-28)

Scope note: Deepstash's Google Play listing could not be fetched directly (page returned truncated/empty content on three attempts), and APKMirror returned HTTP 403, so Android version-history and store numbers below come from third-party trackers and are flagged as such. The Apple App Store listing, deepstash.com, app.deepstash.com/about and app.deepstash.com/curators were fetched directly. All "observed" dates are 2026-10-28 unless stated.

## Key Question 1: What is the core content unit, how is content produced, and what does the catalog claim?

### Takeaway
The atomic unit is the "idea": a short (roughly 200 words or fewer) card with a source (book, article, podcast, video). Ideas are grouped into user folders called "stashes" and, since 2025-2026, into structured book pages (8-15 "Main Ideas", each with 3-6 story cards + takeaway + quiz) and goal-based "Journeys". Content is produced by a mix of community "stashers", an official Curators Programme with in-house content managers, and (per the CEO) AI assistance; the company claims 200,000+ ideas, 5K+ books, 100+ journeys and 50+ topics.

### Cited Findings
- Deepstash describes itself as "a platform for finding and organizing the ideas that matter to you"; ideas are "short, atomic, and self-contained notes", each with a source (book, article, podcast). Ideas are curated by "stashers" (community members) who condense insights into short cards; users can apply to the official Curators Programme — [app.deepstash.com/about](https://app.deepstash.com/about)
- About page figures (observed 2026-10-28): "more than 200,000 ideas", "100+ learning journeys across more than 50 topics", "3M+ Installs" / "Join 3 Million Stashers", App Store 4.8 from 14,500+ reviews, Google Play 4.6 from 92,000+ reviews (these store figures are stale relative to the live App Store listing, see KQ3) — [app.deepstash.com/about](https://app.deepstash.com/about)
- Homepage marketing claims (observed 2026-10-28): "Access to 200,000+ ideas", "200,000+ sources", "5K+" books, "10M+" downloads, "#1 Microlearning App Worldwide", "Loved by 10M+ thinkers". Note the "3M+ Installs" on the About page conflicts with the "10M+" on the homepage — [deepstash.com](https://deepstash.com/)
- Homepage topic labels: Personal Development, Motivation & Inspiration, Productivity, Psychology, Education, Habits, Health; "Collections" section examples include "Soft Resilience" and "Inner Strength" — [deepstash.com](https://deepstash.com/)
- App Store description: short "idea cards" distilled from popular books, paired with artwork; collections ("stashes") to organize saved ideas; free tier includes "Access to 200,000+ ideas" plus the ability to "create and share your own" — [Apple App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/deepstash-smarter-every-day/id1445023295)
- Current book-summary structure (reviewer, Oct 4 2026): book summaries of 8-15 "Main Ideas"; each idea has 3-6 story cards, a key-takeaway explanation and a short quiz; some titles are labelled "From the community" and are plain collections of information cards without that structure; "Journeys" are collections of ideas built around a goal (e.g., "How To Be Good at Parties") and can belong to multiple topics — [Little Almanack review](https://www.littlealmanack.com/p/deepstash-review)
- A competitor review (edgi, Sept 30 2026) counted 212 book pages on the web with 5-15 ideas each (median 10, about 2,187 ideas total), each page listing reading time as "3-5 min"; topic pages also show community-written ideas — [edgi.tv review](https://edgi.tv/reviews/deepstash)
- Headway's review (Jan 31 2026, updated Oct 20 2026) says idea cards are "usually about 200 words or fewer", and per Deepstash's FAQ "users and selected creators write idea cards"; community upvotes raise the strongest cards. (Competitor source; treat comparative claims with caution) — [Headway blog](https://makeheadway.com/blog/deepstash-review/)
- Curators Programme: curators "extract the essence of existing content or share original thoughts as ideas"; benefits include a dedicated Deepstash Content Manager, one-on-one support, performance analytics, algorithmic promotion, inclusion in collections/home-screen features, early access/beta testing; process is sign-up, intro call, content-manager assignment, then publishing via app or website — [app.deepstash.com/curators](https://app.deepstash.com/curators)
- CEO Vladimir Oane (Ness Labs interview, images dated Jan 2024): ideas are public by default and carry topics/metadata; stashes are user-made folders; "over 200,000 ideas", "over 100,000 testimonials"; productivity and personal growth are the most popular topics; he refers to a "recently released" set of "AI companions" and to new AI features for polishing writing and suggesting images — [Ness Labs interview](https://nesslabs.com/?p=14728)
- Deepstash positions ideas as "curated, not summarised" (company framing in 2021-2022-era coverage) — [search summary of promotional sources](https://www.mactrast.com/2022/12/mactrast-deals-deepstash-bite-sized-knowledge-pro-plan/amp/)
- Third-party AI-generated review summary says users criticise content as "superficial, unscientific, and often AI-generated" (weak, unverified signal) — [spark.mwm.ai listing](https://spark.mwm.ai/en/apps/deepstash-smarter-every-day/1445023295)

### Inferences
- Deepstash now runs a two-layer content model: a professionally structured "book" layer (Main Ideas + story cards + quiz, with French/Spanish translations) that looks editorially produced, sitting on top of the legacy community/curator "idea" layer, which is explicitly labelled "From the community".
- The "200,000+ ideas" figure has been static since at least early 2024 (Ness Labs) through Oct 2026 (homepage), suggesting it is a rounded marketing anchor rather than a live count.
- The "10M+" homepage figure most likely counts cumulative downloads across both stores; third-party trackers put Google Play alone at roughly 4-5M installs (KQ3), so 10M+ is plausible but unverified.

### Gaps
- No official statement found on whether AI writes or drafts the structured book summaries; the CEO's "AI companions" remark could not be tied to any documented feature.
- No current official count of curators, books or journeys beyond "5K+ books" and "100+ journeys".

## Key Question 4: Gamification and habit mechanics

### Takeaway
Deepstash uses daily reading goals, streaks with milestone rewards (special app icons, profile badges), a daily reminder time chosen during onboarding, and (since Sept 2026) per-idea quizzes and a reading-progress profile. No feature called "Deepstash Day" was found.

### Cited Findings
- About page lists "daily reading goals and streaks" and public profiles — [app.deepstash.com/about](https://app.deepstash.com/about)
- Screensdesign: streaks ("Dedication pays off") gamify daily usage, rewarding consistency with milestones such as unlocking special app icons or profile badges; onboarding includes a notification-permission screen with a daily reminder time slider — [Screensdesign showcase](https://screensdesign.com/showcase/deepstash-smarter-every-day)
- Onboarding reminder slider at 01:32 changes background from sun to moon; notification permission request at 01:40 — [Screensdesign showcase](https://screensdesign.com/showcase/deepstash-smarter-every-day)
- 32.7.0 (Sep 19 2026) added reading progress to profile with cards and a list of finished/current books; 32.8.0 (Sep 30 2026) added quizzes — [Apple App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/deepstash-smarter-every-day/id1445023295)
- Each book "Main Idea" ends with a short quiz (reviewer) — [Little Almanack review](https://www.littlealmanack.com/p/deepstash-review)
- Nibble lists streaks as the only gamification and mentions no daily goals or reminders (contradicts About page) — [Nibble review](https://nibble-app.com/blog/deepstash-review)
- Social mechanics: follow curators, upvote cards, save counts shown on ideas, curator follower counts — [Nibble review](https://nibble-app.com/blog/deepstash-review); [Screensdesign showcase](https://screensdesign.com/showcase/deepstash-smarter-every-day)

### Inferences
- The habit loop is: onboarding reminder time -> push notification -> daily feed -> read ideas to hit daily goal -> streak milestone (icon/badge). Quizzes and progress tracking added in Sept 2026 extend this toward completion mechanics per book.

### Gaps
- No official documentation of how the daily goal is defined (ideas per day vs minutes), streak-freeze rules, or the full list of achievements/badges.
- "Deepstash Day" returned no results; it may not exist or may be an internal/notification label.

## Key Question 6: Notable product pivots over time

### Takeaway
Deepstash began (2019-2022) as a social "knowledge discovery" network where users ("stashers") clipped and published ideas from articles/podcasts into personal "stashes", with a web clipper and web app; around 2022 ("Deepstash 2.0") it emphasised books/podcasts/videos, by early 2024 it added AI creator tools, and by 2025-2026 it had become a structured book-summary/microlearning product (Main Ideas, story cards, quizzes, Journeys, audio, localisation) with the community layer relegated to "From the community" labels. Users on the App Store note that recent updates reduced standalone quotes and varied content in favour of book summaries.

### Cited Findings
- 2021: "a knowledge discovery social platform for curating content online"; users ("stashers") distil key concepts from sources; core format "idea" saved into "stashes"; "over 1 million users"; 30+ team members, remote; co-founders Vladimir Oane (CEO), Dan Ciotu, Cristian Mezei; future plans "more professional content and collaboration features" — [TechRound, Aug 24 2021](https://techround.co.uk/interviews/meet-vladimir-oane-deepstash/)
- Oane previously co-founded uberVU (sold to HootSuite, 2014); based in Bucharest — [Crunchbase](https://crunchbase.com/person/vladimir-oane); [The Recursive](https://therecursive.com/?p=7735)
- 2020: accepted into Google for Startups Accelerator: Europe "to grow a Knowledge Discovery Network" — [PRWeb release](https://www.prweb.com/releases/deepstash-joins-google-s-startup-accelerator-to-grow-a-knowledge-discovery-network-851818301.html)

### Inferences
- The trajectory is from a Pocket/Pinterest-style UGC clipping network (web clipper, public stashes, curators) toward a Blinkist/Headway-style editorial microlearning subscription; the Curators Programme page (content managers, "produce content with the Deepstash team") marks the midpoint where UGC was professionalised.
