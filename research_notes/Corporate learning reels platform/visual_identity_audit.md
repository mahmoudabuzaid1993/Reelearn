## Saudi/GCC B2B SaaS branding conventions

- **Bilingual lockups are the norm.** MBZUAI's 2026 guidelines separate English-only, Arabic-only and bilingual logos with left-, centre- and right-aligned bilingual variants, and require the lockup to match the asset's language (staticcdn.mbzuai.ac.ae brand PDF). Arabic typically sits right or above in bilingual stacks.
- **Matched Arabic/Latin wordmarks** are a recognised craft: 29LT's logo portfolio (29lt.com/logos) pairs each Arabic wordmark with a drawn Latin counterpart rather than a system font; Creative Bloq called the "Saudi Now" identity the best bilingual wordmark it had seen.
- **Colour:** green/teal (Vision 2030, government, HRDF) and gold/sand are heavily used by Saudi public-sector and semi-government brands; Gulf HR-tech leans purple/violet (Bayzat; Udemy/7taps overlap). Unverified generalisation.
- **Fonts:** Tajawal, Cairo, Almarai and IBM Plex Sans Arabic are the common free bilingual UI pairings (general knowledge; not surfaced by search).

## White space — 5 bullets

1. **Purple/violet is crowded** (7taps, SafetyCulture/Mitti, Udemy, Bayzat): avoid as a primary.
2. **Blue is institutional** (LinkedIn, Coursera) and **green/teal is governmental** in KSA (HRDF/Doroob, Vision 2030): both read "incumbent", not "fresh".
3. **Orange is taken by the two closest frontline competitors** (Axonify, Arist): a warm palette must shift to coral/terracotta/amber or pair with an unexpected secondary.
4. **Open territory:** a high-contrast dark-mode base (TikTok-like) with a single saturated accent that is *not* purple/orange/green (e.g. electric lime-yellow, hot coral, or warm sand on near-black), plus an Arabic-first wordmark. None of the learning competitors has a credible Arabic wordmark; Almentor and Udemy localise UI only.
5. **Shape/tone gap:** competitors are either corporate wordmarks (Axonify, Arist, Mitti) or consumer-playful mascots (Duolingo). A vertical 9:16 "reel frame" or play-glyph integrated into a matched Arabic/Latin wordmark, with a confident-but-warm tone, is unoccupied in this set.
